Typography Stress Tests — Scoped Permissions (ELP_034)

Long-word display type at 320/360/390 containers, with and without content-scoped grants. A heading must wrap at word boundaries or overflow visibly — it must never fracture mid-word.

The field-report case. No typographic permission reaches this heading.
