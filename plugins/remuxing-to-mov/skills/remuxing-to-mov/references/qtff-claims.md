# QTFF claim inventory (Phase 0 of QTFF-AUDIT-PLAN.md)

Every container-level or QuickTime-behavior assertion this plugin makes, stated
as a falsifiable sentence, with the most specific page of the local Apple QTFF
archive (`/Users/salvatore/quicktime-docs/`) it must be resolved against and a
concrete probe. **A divergence is a defect until every probe is individually
explained** (the post-mortem acceptance doctrine). Verification happens in
Phases 1–5; every row starts UNVERIFIED — no row may be flipped without probe
evidence recorded in the commit that flips it.

Paths in *Stated in* are relative to `skills/remuxing-to-mov/`
(`../mov/SKILL.md` is the companion `/mov` skill). Paths in *Spec anchor* are
relative to `/Users/salvatore/quicktime-docs/` (all verified to exist);
"player behavior — empirical only" marks claims the file-format spec cannot
decide.
